About TransNova

TransNova Logistics started in 1988 as a two-vessel feeder operator out of Rotterdam. Today we run weekly loops to eleven ports, with our own customs team and warehouses in Rotterdam and Gdynia.

We are ISO 9001 and AEO-F certified, and we publish our on-time performance every quarter.

Offices

  • Rotterdam (head office)
  • Gdynia
  • Riga
