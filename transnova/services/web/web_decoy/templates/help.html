Help and access

How do I get a portal account?

Accounts are created by your TransNova account manager once your company's framework agreement is signed. Email partners@transnova-logistics.example with your EORI number.

I forgot my password

For security reasons passwords are reset by our service desk only. Call +31 10 555 0142 (Mon-Fri 08:00-18:00 CET).

Which file types can I upload?

PDF, PNG or JPEG customs documents up to 960 KB.

Is there an API?

Yes. Partner API v1 is documented in the onboarding pack; v2 access is by request.
